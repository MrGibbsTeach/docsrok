# The first four: contact routes and ready-to-send messages

Researched 14 September 2026, revised 17 September 2026 after dropping
Safe-R Outcomes and swapping in StackPick (see "Changed since first draft"
below).

Only one of the four publishes an email address. The other three are contact
forms, so these are written short, because form messages get skimmed and
nobody reads a five-paragraph pitch in a text box.

Send them from your Docs Rok address (`hello@docsrok.com`) where an address is
asked for. Sign off with a first name only.

Tag each URL so the analytics can tell you which one worked. The tagged links
are already written into each message below.

---

## Changed since first draft

**Dropped: Safe-R Outcomes.** They sell WHS/SWMS compliance documents
directly — the same broad "documents for tradies" category Docs Rok is in,
just a different subcategory. A business selling documents has no real
incentive to send traffic to another document seller, adjacent or not, and
the fact their list explicitly invites submissions doesn't change that
calculus. Correctly called out and cut rather than sent on hope.

**Also checked and dropped: BackOnTools** (was flagged in
`outreach-roundup-sites.md` as a possible fifth target). It turned out to be
a vendor site selling its own job-management software, not the tools-roundup
content site the original research thought it was — there's no actual
article to submit to. Worth noting as a reminder to verify a target's current
site before sending, not just trust research from a few weeks earlier.

**Added: StackPick** (stackpick.com.au) to bring the list back to four. It's
a genuine affiliate/review site (independent reviews of MYOB, Xero,
ServiceM8, Tradify and similar; monetises via affiliate links, sells nothing
of its own) with a dedicated "Tradies & Builders" section — no document-sales
overlap at all, which is exactly the profile worth prioritising now.

**Reordered** so the safest (least competitive overlap) targets go first:
Melbourne AI Agents and Trade Page, neither of which sell anything
document-adjacent, now lead. StackPick is genuinely safe too but newer to the
list. Chippie stays last — it's a tradie app, closer to competing territory
than the other three, same reasoning as before.

## What to expect

None of these four asked for submissions the way Safe-R Outcomes did, so
treat this as a slightly colder batch than the original plan assumed — one
reply out of four would be a reasonable outcome, not a sign anything is
wrong.

Do not chase. If nobody replies in two weeks, revisit `outreach-roundup-sites.md`
for the remaining candidates (stackpick's sibling sites, tradieautomate.com),
but check each one's current site first — as BackOnTools showed, a roundup
article you found weeks ago may not exist any more.

Check Vercel Analytics for the `?ref=` tags in a fortnight. A listing that
sends nobody is worth knowing about too, because it tells you these roundups
are not the channel and the time is better spent on the search pages — or on
the alternative channels below.
